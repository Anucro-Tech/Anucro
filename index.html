curing, or both.

Each station can run the same recipe or a different one, so two operators can work on separate assembly tasks at the same time.

Dispense onlyCure onlyDispense + cure

From recipe to assembly.

  1. 01 / SET UP

    Select your recipe.

    Choose the saved recipe for the job and bring your process settings to the station.

  2. 02 / DISPENSE

    Press the pedal.

    Trigger the programmed dispense with the foot pedal, keeping your hands free to position the part and apply adhesive.

  3. 03 / CURE

    Position and cure.

    Place the bond area in the curing cup. Start the configured UV exposure using the part sensor or foot switch, according to the selected workflow.

  4. 04 / REPEAT!

    On to the next part.

    Keep the assembly moving. Each operator works through the next part using their station’s selected recipe.

Training at the station

Multiple dispensing modes

Fixed

Set the dispense time and run. Use the same programmed duration for each cycle.

Adjustable

Set a starting dispense time, then adjust it on the fly as your process requires.

Calibrated interpolation

Calibrate an adhesive bottle and save its profile in the library. Exacta Duo uses that profile to adjust dispensing during the run, helping keep deposits consistent from part to part.

Manual

Dispense and cure on demand, with adjustments as you work. For the moments when R&D can’t wait.

System overview

The system, at a glance.

One touchscreen controller connects two dispensing stands, curing cups, and foot switches. Configure each station for its assembly task, with the flexibility to adapt the hardware and workflow as your application develops.

StationsTwo independent operator stations
DispensingElectrically driven, positive-displacement dispensing with position feedback
CartridgesStandard 10 mL and 30 mL setups; custom adapters and saved cartridge profiles for other barrels
UV curing365 nm and 405 nm cup configurations; adjustable exposure time and LED power
OperationFixed, Adjustable, Calibrated Interpolation, and Manual modes; sensor- or pedal-triggered curing
InterfaceBuilt-in touchscreen with external display mirroring
Records & guidanceSaved calibration data, lot history, and recipe-linked training videos
UtilitiesElectrical power; no compressed-air supply required

Cartridge fit, curing cup configuration, and process settings are selected for the application. Talk with us about your barrel, part geometry, and adhesive.
